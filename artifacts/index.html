Verify

Check the signature of an artifact with cosign:

cosign verify \
  --certificate-identity-regexp '^https://github\.com/lastFrake/m1cro\.cloud\.internal/\.github/workflows/publish\.yml@refs/tags/' \
  --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com \
  ghcr.io/lastfrake/m1-core:<VERSION>
